Our Story
The inspiring journey of Firstbörn
🧼
Dr. D.J. James
Founder & Spiritual Husband

In 1967, David Jonathan James started a small commune of people on unoccupied land in the Maine countryside. After David was self-awarded a doctorate and pronounced the spiritual husband of all inhabitants in the commune, a problem presented itself.

The physical manifestation of divine love can become overpowering at times, and so David invented Firstbörn — a soap first designed as both a ritualistic post-coital scrub, a source of fuel, and also an internal elixir for troubled hearts.

Firstbörn exists today as an ephemeral memory of Dr. D.J. James' love for the natural earth and as a testament to the settlers of Commune 1, many of whom gave their lives to the early soap explorations of Dr. James.
